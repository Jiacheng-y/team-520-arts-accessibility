## Tracking and learning

Use opaque codes m001–m030 in the dashboard. Keep contact details separately in an authorized team record. Mark sent only after sending. Record replies, meetings, and bounces manually. A signup is not a reply. One follow-up after roughly a week is sufficient for this round; respect opt-outs.

Ask: How often does disengagement arise? What have you tried? Who owns the budget? How would a story layer fit alongside existing guides? What must a pilot demonstrate? Agree on a real scope and price before interpreting interest as purchase intent.
