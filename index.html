A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before you
step inside.

Is this available to use today?

Not yet. Beyond the Frame is an early-stage concept. We’re collecting interest and speaking with museum teams to shape a first pilot.

Does this replace our existing guide?

The proposed experience adds story-led missions to an existing exhibition. We want to learn how it could complement your current interpretation and education programming.

What would a pilot involve?

We’d discuss a small selection of artworks, the needs of your school groups, and how to evaluate engagement together. Scope, timing, and pricing would be agreed before any commitment.

Do visitors need special equipment?

The concept is designed for phones. Device access, connectivity, shared use, and accessibility needs would be explored with each museum before a pilot.

Can I join if I don’t work at a museum?

Absolutely. Select “A museum visitor” or “An educator” in the form. We’ll keep visitor interest separate from museum pilot inquiries.
